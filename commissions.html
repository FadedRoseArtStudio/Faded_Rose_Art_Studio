Commissions

Art Inspired by You

A meaningful photograph, a familiar face, or an idea you have imagined can be the starting point for a custom piece. A commission offers a way to celebrate someone special, remember a moment, or bring a personal touch to your space.

Explore My Artwork for examples of my work, then consider the subject and style you have in mind.

Ideas for Your Custom Artwork

Starting points for planning a commission
Artwork Idea Personal Inspiration What to Prepare
Portraits Celebrate a loved one or preserve a meaningful expression. A clear reference photo and the number of people you would like included.
Animals & Pets Explore an idea inspired by a beloved pet or a favorite animal. Reference photos and details about the features you want highlighted.
Creative Subjects Consider a favorite object, a seasonal theme, or an imaginative subject. A description of your idea, visual references, and any color preferences.

Planning Your Request

  1. Choose your subject. Think about what you would like the artwork to capture and why it matters to you.
  2. Gather your references. Choose clear photos that show the details you would like included.
  3. Consider the details. Make a note of your preferred size, style, budget, and any date you have in mind.
  4. Discuss the possibilities. Availability, pricing, materials, and timing need to be confirmed before a project begins.
