Choosing Your Options

When imagining a custom piece, think about the details that matter most to you:

  • Subject: A person, animal, favorite object, or imaginative idea.
  • Appearance: Soft shading, strong contrast, color, or a more playful approach.
  • Size: Consider where you would like to display the finished artwork.
  • References: Gather clear photographs or examples that help explain your vision.
