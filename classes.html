Classes

Make Time to Create

Explore your creativity, practice new techniques, and discover the possibilities of making art. This page introduces topics for future classes at Faded Rose Art Studio.

Class dates, pricing, age groups, and registration details will be added as offerings are confirmed.

Drawing Foundations

Explore how simple lines and shapes can become a finished drawing. Topics include observing a subject, sketching proportions, and building a composition.

Light, Shadow & Texture

Discover how shading creates depth. Practice light and dark values, blending, and marks that suggest different surfaces and textures.

Portraits & Expression

Explore the details that give a face its character. Topics include facial proportions, expressive features, and using reference photographs to guide a portrait.

Visit My Artwork to see examples of portrait drawings.

Exploring Color

Experiment with color combinations, layering, and highlights. Everyday objects and playful subjects can provide inspiration for exploring how colors work together.

Creative & Seasonal Projects

Explore ideas inspired by animals, imagined characters, and seasonal themes while practicing drawing skills.

Before You Join

Each confirmed class listing will explain the project, intended experience level, materials, location or format, and cost.

Until those details are available, browse Art Styles & Options for inspiration.
